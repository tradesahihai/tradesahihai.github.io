Today's Market Concepts & Learning Logs

Educational Hub

🎯 1. Central Pivot Range (CPR) Secrets

The Central Pivot Range (TC, Pivot, BC) measures market equilibrium. A Virgin CPR acts as a powerful magnet. When CPR is narrow, anticipate a high-velocity trending day. When wide, expect sideways range-bound swings.

Rule: Buy when price opens above CPR and retests TC with bullish wick rejection. Stop loss below BC.

📊 2. Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) & Traps

Institutions use VWAP as their benchmark execution price. Buying far above VWAP is statistically unfavorable (overbought). Look for mean reversion or VWAP bounces with 20 EMA confluence.

Rule: Best intraday entries occur within 0.2% - 0.4% band distance from the anchored Session VWAP.

🛡️ 3. The 1% Risk of Ruin Mathematics

Never risk more than 1% to 1.5% of total account capital per trade. If your stop loss is ₹30 on a ₹2,500 stock, your maximum position size with a ₹1,00,000 account risking ₹1,000 is 33 shares (₹1,000 / ₹30).

Rule: Position Size = (Capital × Risk %) ÷ (Entry Price - Stop Loss). Never exceed this formula!

🕯️ 4. Multi-Timeframe Top-Down Confluence

Always align your intraday setup with the Daily and 1-Hour trend direction. Trading in the direction of the higher timeframe trend boosts setup win-rate from ~45% to over 65%.

Rule: Daily: Trend Bias | 1-Hour: Key S/R Levels | 5-Minute: Precision Trigger & Stop Loss.
